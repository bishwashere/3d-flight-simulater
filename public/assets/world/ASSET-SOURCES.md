# Level 1 asset sources

Level 1 does not use downloaded maps, satellite imagery, 3D models, textures, or
audio files.

- World geometry and the aircraft are assembled from Babylon.js primitives.
- Materials use project-defined colors and runtime shading.
- Crash fire, smoke, and checkpoint effects are procedural particle effects.
- Engine, wind, checkpoint, crash, fire, and completion sounds are synthesized
  in the browser with the Web Audio API.

There are no external Level 1 art or audio assets requiring attribution.
